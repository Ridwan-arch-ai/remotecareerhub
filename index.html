Stay Safe Online

Protect yourself when looking for remote work.

🔐 Protect Your Information

Avoid sharing passwords, banking information or unnecessary personal information with unknown people.

⚠️ Watch For Scams

Be careful with jobs that promise unusually large amounts of money for very little work or ask you to pay upfront.

🔎 Research Employers

Research a company and verify its official website and contact information before accepting an opportunity.
